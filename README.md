# NordRetail GmbH — H1 2025 Financial Performance Review

## Project Overview
Independent financial controller case study built around a fictional 
e-commerce company operating across three European markets (Germany, 
Poland, Netherlands). The project simulates a real-world H1 management 
reporting cycle — covering variance analysis, KPI monitoring, and 
strategic recommendations.

> *The company, financial data, and business context are illustrative 
> and do not represent any real entity.*

---

## Business Context
**Company:** NordRetail GmbH — D2C e-commerce retailer, Hamburg  
**Markets:** Germany (87.8%), Netherlands (6.4%), Poland (5.8%)  
**Period:** January – June 2025 (H1 Actuals)  
**Benchmarks:** Budget 2025 | Prior Year H1 2024  
**Scale:** ~EUR 341M full-year budgeted revenue  

---

## Key Findings

- **Revenue** of EUR 138.0M came in EUR 22.6M (-14.1%) below budget — 
no single month met the target, with April recording the largest 
deviation at -30.5%
- **Gross Margin** held at 39.3% vs. 40.0% budget (-0.7pp) — confirming 
the issue is volume-driven, not structural
- **EBITDA** of EUR 2.4M missed budget by 72% (EUR -6.1M) due to 
operating leverage: Labour and Warehouse costs did not flex with 
lower revenues
- **Labour/Revenue** ratio deteriorated from 5.7% (budget) to 6.9% 
(actual) — the single largest identifiable cost driver of the EBITDA gap
- **Warehouse/Revenue** ratio ran 1.2pp above budget, consistent with 
minimum-capacity contract commitments

color-coded variances

cost-to-revenue ratios monthly
- **Charts** — Revenue trend, margin % trends, EBITDA bar chart, 
cost structure breakdown, revenue by market

6 strategic recommendations
